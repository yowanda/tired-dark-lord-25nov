A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired.

`main.ts` writes the monologue deterministically to `entry.txt`: Malachar, dark lord of the Ashen Marches for four centuries, receives the prophesied hero in a throne room that has quietly fallen apart — a loose third step, a moth-eaten banner, a sleeping raven, a crown that no longer fits — and lays out his scheme (open the gate beneath the mountain on the eclipse, let the old gods walk out, begin again) while deflecting the hero's sword with a kettle and a cup of tea. The tiredness lives in the rhythm, the pauses and the small deflections, never in a plain statement.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/tired-dark-lord-out node main.ts
```
